Support

SMART Notebook Converter turns PDFs and images into SMART Notebook files, and lets you add annotations to compatible existing notebooks.

Getting started

  1. Open a PDF, image, or .notebook file from your computer or Google Drive.
  2. Choose a resolution when converting a PDF or image. Use the editor tools to add highlights, pen marks, or text to an existing notebook.
  3. Download the result or choose Save to Drive. Keep the original file and review the result before using it.

Google Drive

Drive access is optional. When you choose a Drive action, Google asks you to authorize access to the selected file or to files the app creates. You can revoke that access in your Google Account at any time.

Need help?

Contact your Google Workspace administrator with the file type, browser, and a short description of the issue. Do not send sensitive documents unless you are comfortable sharing them.
