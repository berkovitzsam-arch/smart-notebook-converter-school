Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

SMART Notebook Converter is a browser-based tool that converts PDFs and images into SMART Notebook files and lets users add annotations to existing SMART Notebook files.

Files and content

Conversion and editing happen in your browser. The app does not operate a server that receives, stores, or reviews your documents or annotations.

If you choose Google Drive features, Google authorizes the app through its OAuth flow. The app uses the permissions needed to work with files you choose in the app or files the app creates. File data is downloaded from Google Drive to your browser and, when you choose Save to Drive, uploaded directly from your browser to Google Drive.

Account and authorization data

The app never receives your Google password. Google issues a short-lived access token to your browser session for requested Drive actions. The app does not store that token in a database or use it after the browser session ends.

Third-party services

Google provides the sign-in, Google Picker, and Google Drive services. This site is served by GitHub Pages. Those providers may process technical information such as device, browser, and request data under their own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app changes. School users can direct questions to their Google Workspace administrator.
